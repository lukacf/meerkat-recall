# Contact fact lineage and independent support

Canonical writes store an optional private `_contact_lineage` member inside the
existing `source_commit_receipt` payload. Public `CanonicalCommitReceipt`
serialization omits this member. It holds the actual transaction's Rel/Entity
effects, original relational dependencies, and original source support snapshots.
Independent public edits remain independent for inverse processing; they do not
become source-derived decisions merely to retain contact proof.

Every canonical support write or refresh records its actual `last_commit_id`.
Historical rows without that field remain unknown. Contact eligibility follows
this exact pointer and original input pointers, bounded to 128 visited nodes.
It checks exact row fingerprints, space, current published source revisions,
actual committed endpoint redirects, policy/provenance preservation, and the
original completed acceptance's sealed contact plan and finalization receipt.
A new plain acceptance of the same revision cannot borrow an older contact seal.
Neither a matching fingerprint nor a chronological history search establishes
contact authority.

Retraction, erasure and source replacement can invalidate a receipt which also
last updated an independently supported survivor. Their preparation follows the
actual pointer path before invalidation. An exact retained original support row
is restored when its original fact and final fingerprint match. Otherwise, a
private continuation receipt projects only the surviving fact and exact original
completed publication fence/contact digest, bound to the final surviving row's
full fingerprint. The root must have independently admitted that structured fact;
a derived-only source cannot acquire a new root through withdrawal. Existing
admission capability and availability of a historical contact plan remain separate
(see `source-contact-admission-capability.md`).

Continuation checks compare original raw operation, contact plan, completion,
receipt, head and support control payloads inside the inverse transaction. The
caller also guards its original canonical epoch. Native control guards do not
recheck that epoch after the caller has rotated it. Source replacement attaches
verified roots to its actual new canonical receipt before retiring predecessors;
read-only replacement preparation does not write continuations.

Erasure receives the exact future native serialization of all continuation
receipt and support rows. Its ordinary identity/reference and historical journal
closure checks apply to these rows before writing. There are no new table
exemptions, erased before-images, manufactured assertions, or erased receipt
pointers in the continuation. Live byte-identical survivors are included when
their old pointer is deleted. Missing historical support remains unknown, and
noncontact bookkeeping never invents factual eligibility.

Validation status: source authored, not executed in the integrity worktree.
`source_contact_lineage_tests.rs` covers private/public codec separation, legacy
absence, changed-ID redirect and policy boundaries, actual canonical receipt
rollback, and exact legacy raw-control CAS on both backends. Finalization and
independent qualification own actual published two-source erasure, same-revision
plain reacceptance, independent edits, and post-erasure maintenance oracles. This
change requires the erasure continuation consumer and maintenance contact-lineage
consumer before central validation.

The inverse also protects the surviving source's independently asserted contact
fields before a source-exclusive creation would be deleted. The candidate comes
from an actual invalidated canonical publication and its original completed
operation, sealed contact plan, immutable source, completion, and current support
ledger/writer. I's pure projector reconstructs only that source's admitted fields
on the original selected physical IDs. A source which explicitly depended on the
withdrawn source is not independent.

A applies E's pure composition at the exact earliest invalidated decision/effect
for each physical row. Its commit ID, sequence and full effect are bound. All
other derived inverses retain their ordinary conjunctive behavior. The helper
receives the minimal positive contribution as a baseline before inversion, not a
whole inherited after-image or an instruction to resurrect a deleted row. Later
independent edits and older independent baselines remain subject to the existing
fieldwise inverse rules. Full original control guards travel through lifecycle
and canonical replacement and execute before any inverse effects. Survival and
continuation guard bindings have separate namespaces in the same transaction.

This follow-up remains dependent on I's original contribution projector and E's
composition/lifecycle consumer. F owns the actual first-source-erasure oracle,
including distinct B-owned fields and A-only field removal. Pure routing and
namespace tests are not substitutes for that composed execution.


The source lifecycle carries the exact prepared contact-survival guard from
source decision invalidation into both embedded and native mutation transactions.
Replacement preparation returns that same guard to the canonical writer; no
read-only preparation or pure composition writes new authority. Guard checks
precede inverse, replacement, support and receipt writes.

At the earliest invalidated effect for a physical contact record, the pure
composer substitutes only independently admitted positive fields into the
surviving baseline. The ordinary three-way inverse removes unsupported fields
and preserves later independent edits. Original live sets prevent a removed
alias or identity key from being reconstructed from an older admission. Required
contributor restrictions remain in the final envelope. Entity type, identity key
endpoint, relationship topology and validity must agree with actual current facts;
ambiguous or unsupported facts remain a typed conflict. Absent or tombstoned
records are never revived. Physical identity and birth metadata remain those of
the actual row, not the source used to construct a minimal contribution.

Pure composition regressions cover distinct source-owned names and keys, removal
of source-exclusive nested metadata and evidence, retained manual names/status,
older independently supported baselines, identity evidence, inherited factual
mismatch, exact decision membership, and missing/deleted originals. These are
not publication-authority fixtures. Actual shared-contact publication, erasure,
private-plan purge, independent survival, fresh reprocessing and maintenance
remain covered by the pipeline's separate complete lifecycle oracles.

Survival additionally follows the current support's actual private writer path
back to the exact candidate original publication. The first publication by that
source must match its original operation/generation/revision; a newer plain
publication cannot borrow an older same-revision projector. Private lineage now
captures EntityIdentityKey and Entity-only bundles as well as relationships, so
this check covers contact admissions without a company edge. Missing historical
paths remain unknown. Two additional actual canonical tests cover an identity-only
bundle and rollback without manufacturing a positive contact root. The original
observed tombstone remains deleted even if an intermediate inverse overlay is live.
